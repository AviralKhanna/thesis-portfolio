# Business-intuition and investment-memo review

Status: processed into proposed rules and analyzer schemas on 2026-08-03.

## Accepted improvements

- Begin with customers, purchase decisions, channels, competition, and business economics before financial outputs.
- Replace generic moat labels with an evidence-led replicability test.
- Expand management evaluation beyond governance facts to execution, incremental returns, guidance, acquisitions, innovation, culture, alignment, succession, disclosure, and mistakes.
- Add explicit second-order reasoning.
- Separate the strongest ownership, rejection, and neutral cases.
- Produce a compact investment memo alongside the complete evidence report.
- Link unanswered questions to decisions, thesis breakers, and monitoring triggers.

## Guardrail retained

Numeric market shares, moat replication cost/time, and scenario probabilities must not be invented. Unknown values remain visible. Scenario probabilities require all cases to be specified, total 100%, and be consciously approved with supporting evidence or calibration history.

## Rule-status decision

The review generated proposed rules only. No adopted rule, weight, threshold, or compatibility score was changed without user approval.
