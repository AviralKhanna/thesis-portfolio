# Professional research review - memory record

## Source

User review dated 3 August 2026 of the first Exide analysis.

## Strengths to preserve

- Separate evidence from opinion.
- Treat management guidance as an unverified claim.
- Explain cash-flow changes rather than rewarding headline growth.
- Penalize missing valuation and peer evidence.
- Treat capital invested as distinct from value created.
- Keep adopted and proposed rules separate.

## Weaknesses identified

- Analysis explains what happened more often than why or what may happen next.
- Business quality, moat, industry structure, and competitive position are underdeveloped.
- Management claims need independent tests and dated promise tracking.
- Governance needs a complete scorecard rather than promoter holding alone.
- Reports need bull/base/bear cases, thesis breakers, ranked risks, and carefully calibrated uncertainty.
- Every material conclusion needs an evidence type and confidence level.
- Personal philosophy compatibility must remain the governing output.

## System decisions resulting from this review

- Added 14 proposed knowledge rules; none were automatically adopted.
- Added structured business, claim, peer, promise, and scenario inputs.
- Added an evidence-confidence engine.
- Expanded analyst instructions to require causality, external verification, moat analysis, management history, and scenario reasoning.
- Added a staged research roadmap.

## Open design decision

The user must review which new proposed rules should become part of the adopted philosophy and assign their weights. Until then, reports may discuss these dimensions but must not include them in compatibility scores.
