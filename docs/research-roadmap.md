# Research-system roadmap

This roadmap converts the 3 August 2026 review into staged engineering work. It prevents attractive but disconnected features from replacing research discipline.

## Level 1 - Financial statement analyzer

Status: working foundation.

- Cross-statement validation and ratio reconciliation
- Variance explanations and one-time-item detection
- Trend classification: improving, deteriorating, volatile, cyclical
- Cash-flow decomposition into operations, working capital, asset sales, and financing
- Confidence: high for audited facts; lower for inferred causes

Exit condition: every material numeric conclusion is reproducible and its cause is either evidenced or marked unresolved.

## Level 2 - Business and moat analyzer

Status: expanded schema and deterministic report sections added; evidence collection required.

- Revenue, product, geography, segment, and customer maps
- Purchase-decision map separating payer, user, influencer, switching, repeat demand, and channel power
- Moat dimensions with economic mechanism, counter-evidence, replication time/cost, and durability tests
- Industry-driver map and market-position history
- Peer-normalized margins, growth, returns, leverage, cash conversion, and valuation
- Second-order chains covering competitor, customer, channel, and long-term responses

Exit condition: the system can answer whether the company is strengthening relative to suitable competitors and why.

## Level 3 - Management and governance evaluator

Status: expanded management schema and proposed rules added.

- Capital-allocation history and incremental-return tracking
- Dated guidance-versus-outcome memory
- Claim verification against independent evidence
- Governance scorecard covering auditors, board, pay, related parties, pledge, dilution, litigation, succession, and controls

Exit condition: credibility is based on delivery history, not management tone.

## Level 4 - Investment intelligence

Status: scenario, adversarial debate, and one-page memo schemas added; probabilities remain human-approved.

- Bull/base/bear cases with catalysts and valuation implications
- Thesis breakers and ranked risks
- Scenario-sensitive valuation and expected-return ranges
- Evidence confidence for every material conclusion

Exit condition: uncertainty and falsification conditions are explicit before a decision.

## Level 5 - Personal decision and learning engine

Status: decision journal exists; calibration layer pending.

- Philosophy compatibility with failed-rule explanations
- Immutable decision snapshots and appended reviews
- Prediction calibration and recurring-mistake detection
- Rule-change recommendations requiring user approval
- Cross-company and portfolio research memory

Exit condition: the system learns from outcomes without rewriting history or changing philosophy autonomously.

## Guardrails

1. Never invent external validation, peers, market share, or scenario probabilities.
2. A missing input is a research question, not permission to guess.
3. Keep proposed rules outside compatibility until explicitly adopted.
4. Confidence measures evidence quality, not optimism.
5. Store contradictions rather than forcing consensus.
